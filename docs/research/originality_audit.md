# Originality Audit

Access date: **2026-11-22**. See competitor_analysis.md for sources.

## Signature mechanic vs. closest precedents

| CONSEQUARRY mechanic | Closest precedent | Overlap | Verdict / change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Consequence objects** (typed, sized, routed packets with ledger + conservation) | Democracy 4 effect web; Factorio item flows | D4 effects are continuous modifiers, not discrete routable objects. Factorio moves goods, not outcomes. | **Distinct.** Keep. Make orbs physical and traceable. |
| **Redirect / Split** along a causal graph | Overflow (signals between nodes, real-time) | Both route "load" between nodes. Overflow: one full node = run over [press release]. | **Moderate risk.** Our routing is turn-based, typed, with per-type vulnerability; capacity overflow spawns new consequences (conservation) rather than ending the run. Loss is multi-system collapse. Re-check when Overflow demo releases (3 Dec 2026). |
| **Delay with interest** | Anachrony time-warp loans and paradoxes | Borrowing against the future with penalty. | **Redesigned before production:** Delay is not a resource loan. It defers a *harm* and adds a separate "Deferred Strain" consequence (25%) that lands with it; max 2 delays per consequence; cannot pass the final turn. Documented in DECISIONS.md D-004. |
| **Transform** (convert Heat→Surplus at Energy etc.) with residue | Factorio recipes; generic crafting | Conversion rules exist everywhere. | Distinct by requiring the consequence to *physically be at* the converting system (redirect-then-transform combo) and by producing a residue consequence elsewhere. |
| **Controlled Crisis** | Frostpunk extreme laws (social cost) | Deliberate harm for gain. | Distinct: crisis spawns concrete consequence orbs that must themselves be routed; escalating cost, cooldown, memory resistance. |
| **World Memory** (resilience, scars, adaptation, dependency, resistance) | Roguelite meta progression (Against the Storm) | Meta progression persists *between* runs; ours is *within* a run and mechanically bidirectional. | Distinct. |
| Autonomous civilization, indirect player | Reus/Reus 2, WorldBox | Indirect god perspective. | Thematic only; we never place buildings or terrain. |
