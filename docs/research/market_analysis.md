# Market Analysis (preliminary)

Access date: **2026-11-22**. **[V]** verified from cited source; **[K]** unverified background knowledge; **[A]** assumption.
Nothing here is a sales forecast. Commercial success is not assumed.

## Steam market conditions

- Steam median revenue per new release in 2025 was reported at about **$249** (second-lowest since 2020) [V: https://80.lv/articles/analysts-report-drop-in-game-revenue-on-steam-despite-growing-number-of-releases]. Another estimate puts the median indie at **$5k–15k lifetime gross** [V: https://steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-revenue-data]. Definitions differ; both say most games earn little.
- ~**55 new titles per day** on Steam in Q1 2026 [V: https://games-stats.com/blog/Steam_in_Q12026/].
- City Builder tag: **436** releases in 2025 vs 342 in 2024; 415 in 2026 by late September [V: https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/city-builder]. → The tag is **saturated**. CONSEQUARRY should *not* position as a city builder; primary tags should be Strategy / Roguelite / Simulation / Turn-Based / Management, with "systemic" hook copy.
- City Builder slipped to No. 7 in GameDiscoverCo's 2025 hit-genre comparison [V: https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-have-the-genres-of-hit-pc-games].
- Median price of best-selling new Steam games dropped ~14% in ~2 years; $10–15 indie titles are the comparison point [V: https://www.gamesradar.com/games/the-median-price-of-best-selling-new-games-on-steam-has-dropped-in-the-past-2-years-research-finds-charging-usd25-is-getting-trickier-as-players-compare-value-to-the-usd10-usd15-indie-titles].
- Deep-system genres show long-tail revenue from loyal players (Timberborn, Going Medieval cited) [V: https://games-stats.com/blog/Steam_in_Q12026/].

## Price hypothesis

- Directive hypothesis **$12.99** is consistent with the $10–15 indie band above [A]. Reference points: Against the Storm $29.99 list (frequently discounted to ~$9) [V: notebookcheck link in competitor_analysis.md]; Terra Nil $24.99 criticised as steep for its length [V: https://nichegamer.com/reviews/terra-nil].
- Recommendation [A]: keep $12.99 hypothesis; validate with demo wishlist conversion before committing. Content target for that price: ≥ 8–10 hours to "seen most systems" plus seeded replay.

## Positioning hypothesis [A]

- One-line pitch: *"A turn-based roguelite where you don't build the city — you decide where its disasters go."*
- Audience overlap: players of Against the Storm, Frostpunk, Democracy, Balatro-style short-run systemic games.
- Creator appeal hooks: visible cascading chains, "I turned a meltdown into a power surplus" moments, shareable seed codes, daily-seed challenge (offline-computable from date).

## Risks

1. Genre saturation of management/city tags (verified above).
2. Systemic-sim opacity risk (Fate of the World precedent) — core retention risk.
3. Abstract premise is harder to sell in a capsule image than a concrete one. Mitigation: strong isometric miniature visuals with glowing consequence orbs.
4. No human playtest data yet. All engagement claims are unvalidated.
