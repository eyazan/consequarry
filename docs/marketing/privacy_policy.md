# CONSEQUARRY — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

CONSEQUARRY does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

- The game works fully offline. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs.
- Your progress, settings and best scores are saved only on your own device.
- "Copy run report" copies a short text summary of a finished run (seed, result, move counts, device type) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it yourself.
- Purchases on mobile are processed by Apple (or Google). We receive no payment details.
