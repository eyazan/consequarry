## Long description (EN)
Accidents, heatwaves, spills and strikes keep hitting your little valley. You can't stop them. **Every consequence must go somewhere — you decide where.**

- **Drag trouble where it hurts least.** Heat scorches farms but barely warms a power plant. Every district has its own weak spots.
- **Turn problems into power.** Capture heat as energy, turn waste into fertiliser — but every fix leaves a residue somewhere else.
- **30 hand-tuned valleys** across River Valley, Dry Hills and Boomtown. Each takes a few minutes; earn up to three stars.
- **New moves as you go:** split a disaster, delay it, or trigger a controlled crisis for a big bonus — at a price the valley remembers.
- **A world that remembers.** Districts harden after repeated hits, processes get better with use, and leaning on the same neighbour wears it down.
- **Daily valley** — the same challenge for everyone, every day.
- Plays offline. No account. No ads. No subscription.
